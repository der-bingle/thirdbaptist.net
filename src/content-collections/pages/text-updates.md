# Church Text Updates

Get church announcements and reminders from **Third Baptist West Frankfort** by text.

## Sign Up

Text **START** to **618-923-9823** to subscribe. On a phone, [tap here to start a text with START filled in](sms:+16189239823?body=START), then send the message.

By texting START, you consent to receive recurring text messages from Third Baptist West Frankfort. Messages may include church announcements, prayer updates, service and event reminders, and schedule or weather changes. **Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.** Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time or **HELP** for help. Consent is not a condition of making a purchase, donation, membership, or participation in church activities.

We do not sell or share mobile opt-in data for promotional or marketing purposes. Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).
